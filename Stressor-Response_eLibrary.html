Tool: Pacific Salmonid Stressor-Response eLibrary (Stressor-Response_eLibrary)


Authors

Danielle Andrews, Paxton Calhoun

Contributors

Aimee Fullerton, Morgan Bond, Sierra Sullivan, Matthew Bayly, Maelice Yamdjieu, Garrett Ringler, Lavanya Bunadri, Ruqhaiya Syeda

Description

The Pacific Salmonid Stressor-Response eLibrary is an open-source, interactive R Shiny application and PostgreSQL database designed to support researchers and modelers working with life cycle models. Its primary motivation is to act as a research and management tool for Pacific salmonid recovery by consolidating quantitative relationships between environmental stressors and salmonid biological response at a specific life stage into a centralized decision- and research-support tool. Users can explore database coverage, dynamically filter relationships, overlay multiple functional curves for comparative analysis, export data, and submit new quantitative relationships for administrative vetting and inclusion.
